# Learning plan: Arjuna and the bird's eye (ages 6–9, Hindi, 2:49)

> FROZEN: audio, script, `word-timestamps.json`, `phrases.json` (see `frozen.sha256`).

## Objectives
After watching, a child can:
1. **Retell the story**: Drona's challenge (hit the wooden bird's eye), who tried, what each one saw, and who succeeded.
2. **Explain एकाग्रता in their own words** as "looking only at your goal and letting everything else go", because they *saw* the world fade away around the eye.
3. **Recognise that showing off and anger (घमंड, गुस्सा) don't help**, while calm effort and good manners do.
4. **Connect it to their own life**: "when I look only at my target, hard things get easier."

## Concept sequence: introduce → reinforce → recall
| Concept | Introduce | Reinforce | Recall / payoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| The target = the bird's **eye** | L4: ring circles the painted eye | L19 (tiny far bird), L27 (eye fills the ring) | L32: the arrow lands exactly there |
| **"What do you see?"** (attention) | L9–10 Duryodhana: wide, jittery POV | L16 Yudhishthira: wide, steady POV | L27 Arjuna: only the eye |
| **Pride / anger don't help** | L5–7 swagger, L11–13 anger | L17 Yudhishthira steps back politely (contrast) | L35 घमंड shown and gently set aside |
| **एकाग्रता** | L21–24 stillness, breath held | L27 world falls away | L36 the unbreakable ring, L37–38 the child uses it |

Healthy repetition: The Question ×3, Step Back ×2, the Focus Ring ×6 (each with a new job).

## Interaction moments
- **Think pause 1** (after L1, 5.76–7.29 s): "Who will be the greatest archer?" The five silhouettes glance at each other, and the bow-shaped "?" glows softly. The answer is revealed over the story.
- **Think pause 2** (after L19, 89.75–90.97 s): "Was it impossible?" A tiny bird sits in a huge sky, with the "?" beside it. The answer comes on L20 when Arjuna steps forward.
- **Implicit prediction** (L26 → L27): after two failures, the child has learned the pattern and anticipates the POV window. The payoff is that it's different this time.

## Recap (end)
The narration has no list-style recap, so the recap is **visual and in order**: on L35–36 we briefly see the same images used earlier.
- Duryodhana's big crown (his L5 pose)
- the wide shaky POV ring (L10)
- Arjuna's locked ring on the eye (L27)

The **same Focus Ring** then carries straight into the child's world (L37–38), so the last image the child sees is *themselves* using the lesson.

## Notes
- The story follows the popular retelling in the script (Duryodhana first, the arrow hits the eye). This was reported in Stage 0 and left unchanged by request.
- Line 35's wording ("सिर्फ़ ताकत या घमंड नहीं") is kept. The visual makes घमंड clearly *not* the way: it deflates, while ताकत is shown as fine but not enough.
